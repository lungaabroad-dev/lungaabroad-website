General education

Understanding Francophone Mobility.

Francophone Mobility is an employer-specific work permit pathway under Canada's International Mobility Program. For eligible cases, employers outside Quebec may hire eligible French-speaking workers without an LMIA. Applicants generally need French speaking and listening skills at NCLC 5 or higher, an eligible job offer, and must meet all other requirements. Primary agriculture occupations under TEER 4 and 5 are excluded.

The employer must submit the offer through the IRCC Employer Portal using exemption code C16 and complete the applicable steps. See current IRCC eligibility ↗

Training scope and immigration eligibility are different matters. Eligibility depends on current IRCC rules and individual circumstances.

Common questions

Partnership FAQ.

Is Lunga Abroad a recruitment agency?

No. Lunga Abroad is a French language education and talent preparation platform. Recruitment and employment decisions remain with authorized agencies and Canadian employers.

Does Lunga Abroad directly provide jobs in Canada?

No. We do not directly provide or guarantee employment. Our role is language education and candidate preparation.

Who can become a Lunga Abroad partner?

Licensed Canadian recruitment agencies, RCICs, immigration lawyers, French language institutions, and other strategic organizations may submit an inquiry.

Can RCICs collaborate with Lunga Abroad?

Yes. We welcome authorized professionals interested in Francophone immigration education and candidate preparation.

What kind of candidates does Lunga Abroad prepare?

Candidates from different education and professional backgrounds through language education, workplace communication, assessment, and career readiness activities.

Does Lunga Abroad train for TEER 0–5?

Language training can be adapted to communication needs across TEER 0–5. That scope does not guarantee immigration eligibility.

Does Lunga Abroad guarantee Francophone Mobility eligibility?

No. Eligibility depends on current IRCC requirements and individual circumstances. We provide education and general information, not immigration approval guarantees.
