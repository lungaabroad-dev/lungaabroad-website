A pathway for eligible employers

A more direct way to explore Francophone talent.

Francophone Mobility is part of Canada's International Mobility Program. Eligible employers outside Quebec may hire eligible French-speaking foreign workers without an LMIA, subject to current IRCC requirements.

ADVANTAGE 01

LMIA-exempt hiring

Eligible C16 Francophone Mobility hires do not require an LMIA application. Employers still need to meet the applicable program and employment requirements.

ADVANTAGE 02

A defined employer process

Eligible employers submit the offer through the IRCC Employer Portal. This removes the LMIA application stage; it does not guarantee faster processing or approval.

ADVANTAGE 03

Different government fee structure

The standard employer compliance fee is CAD $230. An LMIA application fee is not required for eligible C16 hiring. Other costs may still apply.

ADVANTAGE 04

French-trained talent

Explore candidates developing French skills for daily communication, professional settings, and workplace-specific vocabulary.

French training is not proof of a specific NCLC result. Employers should review each candidate's verified language evidence and qualifications.

From hiring needs to application

How Francophone Mobility can work.

A five-step overview for employers. This is general information; confirm the current requirements for your role and candidate.

  1. 01

    Share hiring needs

    Tell us the role, work location, experience, qualifications, wage range, and number of openings.

  2. 02

    Review potential fit

    We may review our learning community for candidates whose background and language preparation appear relevant. Any introduction must comply with applicable laws.

  3. 03

    Interview and decide

    Your organization independently interviews candidates, makes the hiring decision, and issues a genuine offer with clear employment terms.

  4. 04

    Submit through IRCC

    If eligible, the employer submits the offer in the Employer Portal using LMIA exemption code C16 and pays the applicable compliance fee.

  5. 05

    Candidate applies

    The employer shares the offer number and required details. The candidate submits an employer-specific work permit application to IRCC.

Work permit approval is decided by IRCC. Employers remain responsible for genuine job offers, working conditions, program requirements, and accurate documentation.

Important eligibility note

Some primary agriculture roles are excluded.

Under current Francophone Mobility eligibility rules, primary agriculture occupations classified under TEER 4 and TEER 5 are excluded. Other occupation, work location, employer, and candidate requirements also apply.

Employer questions

Francophone Mobility FAQ.

What is Francophone Mobility?

It is an LMIA-exempt work permit pathway under Canada's International Mobility Program for eligible French-speaking workers who will live and work outside Quebec, subject to all applicable requirements.

Do employers need an LMIA?

Eligible employers do not need an LMIA for a qualifying Francophone Mobility C16 offer. They still need to meet applicable requirements, submit the offer through IRCC when required, and pay the employer compliance fee unless exempt.

What are the standard fees?

The standard employer compliance fee is CAD $230. The standard work permit application fee is CAD $155 and is generally paid by the candidate. Other fees may apply; check the current IRCC fee list.

What French level is required?

Eligible candidates generally need speaking and listening proficiency equivalent to NCLC 5 or higher. Employers should review each candidate's relevant language evidence.

Can employers hire across TEER 0–5?

The pathway can cover different TEER categories, but primary agriculture occupations in TEER 4 and TEER 5 are excluded. The actual NOC, job duties, location, employer, and candidate must satisfy current requirements.

Does Lunga Abroad charge for job offers?

No. Lunga Abroad does not charge employers for issuing job offers or sell job offers or work permits.

Does Lunga Abroad guarantee a candidate or work permit?

No. Lunga Abroad does not guarantee candidate availability, employment, or immigration approval. Employers make hiring decisions; IRCC decides work permit applications.
